ATL50 - Leg 6; Azalea Park to Upper Morgan Falls Dam

2013-08-24 · Lester Martin

It was great to get back on the river and put down some more miles on the ATL50 - Canoeing thru the Chattahoochee River NRA journey, if even only 3.5 miles.  It was even greater to get on the water with my beautiful wife.

After leaving all the "kids" behind at Azalea Park (you know the place – 420 Island) we were almost immediately on the river alone.  Well, as usual, we had some ducks to keep us company.

This is a section where the river gets very, very wide.  In fact, they even call a piece of it Bull Sluice Lake and the whole lake river  just slows down.

In fact, at several points we stopped paddling and had absolutely ZERO forward movement from the river current; or lack of it.

Oh, and did we say we saw some ducks?

To our surprise, we stumbled upon some very cool cliffs as well as some "kids"  who thought it would be a good time to jump off them into the river below.  For me, a few pictures were more fun.

This little 3.5 mile trip, captured on our Kayaking & Canoeing Google Map went very smoothly, was a lot of fun, and only took us an hour and 15 minutes.  We got great use out of our portage cart pulling the canoe and gear up the hill at the Morgan Falls Park canoe launch up to the parking lot where we were greeted to a nice view of the river.

This puts the overall 50 mile journey just over the 35 mile mark, so the focus now is to wrap it up (and to enjoy every moment on the river!).
