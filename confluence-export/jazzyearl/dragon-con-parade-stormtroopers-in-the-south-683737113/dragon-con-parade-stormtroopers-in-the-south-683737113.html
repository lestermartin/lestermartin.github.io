Dragon Con Parade - Stormtroopers in the South

2018-09-01 · Lester Martin

While it has been over 10 years since I have lived in Atlanta, this is only the second time I’ve been down to the see the parade that kicks off Dragon Con over the Labor Day weekend. This is just an overwhelming display of cosplay that can’t be missed.

That moment at #DragonCon when you give up guessing what the #CosPlay outfit is in front of you as you realize it is just their normal wear...

— Earls of Wisdom (@EarlsOfWisdom) September 1, 2018

I followed the suggestions in https://365atlantatraveler.com/dragoncon-parade/ and rode MARTA down to the North Avenue station and then just wandered down from the top of the parade route to find a spot. Highly recommended sitting on the east side of the street to ensure you have ample shade.

Also, as that article said, there are over 60,000 people who show up for the parade so get there early so you can get some shade and actually see the parade. Folding camping chairs are a must!!

The parade was great, but I did notice some differences since the last time I went to see the big show.

  • There were only small contingents for the Trekkies and the Steampunks…

  • TONS of folks representing video games

  • Star Wars, Star Wars, Star Wars

    • The widest covered theme by far

    • Boba Fett was the single most represented character in the entire parade

    • Not quite as many Stormtroopers as I remember, but still a large cadre

    • Dancing Lando with his hip-hop Stormtroopers was awesome!

  • Next to Star Wars, the plethora of Deadpool one-offs made this the second widest theme present

  • Lots of little, but well organized, groups covering “old school” themes such as

    • Scoobie Doo

    • Gilligan’s Island

    • He-Man

  • Loved the brigade of Handmaids!!

  • Not as many Aliens folks this time around – SAD!!!!

  • Speaking of “SAD!!!!”, I was amazed, but surely not upset, at how many boo’s were given when MAGA-man went by!!

  • There really were only a handful of classical superheros (Superman, Batman, etc) in the parade

  • Those damn Spartans showed up again making us middle-aged men with guts have to watch all the ladies around google at their six (and sometimes eight) packs

  • My favorite nerdy thing was the Cult of Marriott Carpet (I guess you had to be there)

  • Lastly, I was so happy to see that NOBODY was ashamed of their bodies!!
