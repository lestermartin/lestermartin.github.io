accepting "best answer" on hcc (it is the ~right~ thing to do)

2017-04-10 · Lester Martin

The Hortonworks Community Connection (HCC) is a GREAT resource for folks helping each other out in the big data community with special focus on all things Hadoop (including components like HBase) and Spark.  To help encourage folks to continue to provide answers to questions, you should "Accept" an answer if there is one that best helps you out.  This is just a simple link at the bottom of an answer.

When you do that, the person answering your question will get marked with the "Best Answer".

In this world of gamification, this tiny effort on your part continue to fire-up most folks who are trying to help by helping them build their online "reputation".
