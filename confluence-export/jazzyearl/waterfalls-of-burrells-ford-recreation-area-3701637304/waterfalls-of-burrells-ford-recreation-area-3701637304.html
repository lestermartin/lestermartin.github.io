Waterfalls of Burrells Ford Recreation Area

2025-02-09 · Lester Martin

Ever since visiting some Waterfalls In & Around Greenville, South Carolina I’ve been wanting to see these two falls in nearby Burrells Ford Recreation Area; Spoonauger Falls & King Creek Falls.

Spoonauger Falls

First up was Spoonauger Falls and it took more like 30 minutes to make it to the falls than the 20 that site suggested, but it was clearly worth it. Most of the trail was pretty darn easy, but there were a few little water crossings at the beginning and some slightly technical bits toward the end. All in all, a nice trail.

Before too long, you could see the waterfall peeking out of the trees.

Just a bit further down the path you get the full view and can stand at the base of the falls.

King Creek Falls

The trail down to King Creek Falls was a bit longer, but in reality it was much easier. Well, until the last 100 yards or so were there were a few trail obstacles like this one.

But scrambling over them while not falling down into the valley to the left of the trail was worth it.
