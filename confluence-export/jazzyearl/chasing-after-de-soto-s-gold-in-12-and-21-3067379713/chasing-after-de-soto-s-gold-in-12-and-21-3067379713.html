Chasing After de Soto's Gold in '12 and '21

2021-11-06 · Lester Martin

Nine years later, I find myself back at Desoto Falls up in the Chattahoochee National Forest. The kids brought me last time for a Father’s Day treat in 2012. They look so sweet back then (they STILL ARE!) and I had my braces back still on; I even had some hair!!

This trip today was as much about looking for fall colors as much as seeing the Waterfalls. Most of the leaves were already on the ground, but still got a few nice pictures.

The 100 foot waterfall was incredible!
