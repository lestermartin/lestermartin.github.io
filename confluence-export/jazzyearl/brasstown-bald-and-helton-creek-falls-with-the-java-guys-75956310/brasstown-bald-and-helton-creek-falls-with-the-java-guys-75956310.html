Brasstown Bald and Helton Creek Falls with the Java Guys

2012-11-17 · Lester Martin

In efforts to update my Waterfalls page, this Nov 2012 trip was back-posted in Feb 2016.

In my quest to get out and see as many Georgia Waterfalls as I can and upon finding out that two of my coworkers (and good friends!) had never been up to Brasstown Bald, I suggested an early Saturday morning trip and they somehow went along with it!!  After our drive and relatively short, BUT STEEP, hike we made it to the obligatory marker and the cool tower at the top of this 4784 foot summit.

 

Of course, I was glad to take these scenic postcard pictures for Tony and David.

 

What beautiful views available on this cold November morning.

But as someone who has been to Brasstown a number of times, I was thrilled to drive down the mountain and find the Helton Creek Falls Trail marker.

Just moments from the leaving the car we were greeted by the lower falls; a classic 40 foot slide.

Yes, of course we have time for more postcard pictures!!

   

An unbelievable short walk just up the trail got us to the even bigger upper waterfall; an 80 foot two-tiered drop into a shallow pool.

 

Here's the fellas just to give this beautiful waterfall some scale.

It truly is a beauty!!
