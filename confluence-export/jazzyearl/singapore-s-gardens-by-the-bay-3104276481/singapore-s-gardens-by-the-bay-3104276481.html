Singapore’s Gardens by the Bay

2022-09-07 · Lester Martin

I was lucky enough to visit Singapore - The Lion City before COVID and extremely lucky to visit again. This time, I wanted to make sure I visited the iconic Gardens by the Bay. There are 18 of these famous Supertrees around the grounds.

There are a few other things to do while at the Gardens. I was lucky to check out a couple of the gigantic enclosed structures.

Flower Dome

The Flower Dome was a huge treat. Not only do I love taking pictures of flowers, but they had the AC blasting in there!!

What a great place for a selfie!

It is called the Flower Dome, so I’d better share some flowers like you see in the picture above.

It is hard to visualize just how big this HUGE covered garden is!

Cloud Forest

The Cloud Forest is another gigantic domed building – visions of the original Logan’s Run were dancing through my head! As a lover of Waterfalls, I was glad to encounter this incredible man-made beast just as you walk in!

GardenByBay.MOV

The other thing you notice in a hurry is that there is no AC in this dome. That said, the beauty of it all overwhelms you and you quickly forget about the humidity. Well, mostly forget about it; haha! Let me just show you some pictures to try to show you how cool it really is.

The Supertrees

It was dark when I got back outside and enjoyed the night time perspective of the Supertrees and some of the surrounding iconic images of Singapore.

Goodnight, sweet Singapore.
