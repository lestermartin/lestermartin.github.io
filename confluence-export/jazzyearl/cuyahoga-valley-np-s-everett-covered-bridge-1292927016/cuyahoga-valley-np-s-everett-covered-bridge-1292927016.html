Cuyahoga Valley NP's Everett Covered Bridge

2019-05-16 · Lester Martin

While out checking out Six Cleveland Area Waterfalls in Two Days I was able to visit Cuyahoga Valley National Park which was where I got my latest Covered Bridges fix by visiting Everett Covered Bridge.

Nice lines and condition outside and great “bones” inside.

As you can see, the national park staff keep this covered bridge in tip-top shape.

I wish all of the Covered Bridges I have seen have been kept up this well. I really enjoyed my time visiting this little piece of history.
