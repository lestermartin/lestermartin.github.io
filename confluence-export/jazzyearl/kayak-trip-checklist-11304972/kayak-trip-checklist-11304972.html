Kayak Trip Checklist

2013-07-15 · Gretchen

  • Day before
    • Print-off of maps
    • Ensure ice is available
    • Charge camera battery
  • For car
    • Rack and straps
    • (optional) parking pass
  • Essentials
    • Kayaks (and leg cushions)
    • Paddles
    • Life jackets
    • Cable lock
    • Water shoes and sandals
    • Sunscreen
    • Sunglasses
    • Hat
    • Camera
    • Waterproof bag & boxes
    • Wet wipes
    • Hand sanitizer
    • River map
    • Seats
    • Portage cart
  • Cooler
    • Plenty of ice
    • Bottles of water
    • (optional) food
  • For afterwards
    • Towels
    • Dry clothing (at least, t-shirt)
    • Flip flops
  • Once home
    • Clean and store kayak
    • Wash/vacuum car
    • Blog about it!!
