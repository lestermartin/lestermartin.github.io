Rubi's 1st Field Trial at Sloppy Floyd's

2022-02-27 · Lester Martin

It has been a little while since Introducing Rubi - My Coleman Rubicon and, in fact, I tried to take her out twice before. Both ended before they started due to some brake controller issues that are all sorted out now. Yes, the camper trailer FINALLY got out in the wild. Her first destination was James H. “Sloppy” Floyd State Park.

This park is not all that big, but it really is a great little “secret” state park that really does not get overwhelmed. As the map below shows, there is something for the Waterfalls nuts like me and I was lucky to visit the Marble Mine Trail’s little waterfall on my first visit.

The campground at the southern entrance only has 24 campsites and most of them are pull-throughs which I love. I stayed at Campsite #9 just across from the comfort station.

And yes… I solve the brake controller issue with a new ride! A 2022 VW Atlas!!

It was a cold weekend in the 40’s the whole time. The Lower Lake Trail was a nice break from staying warm in the trailer and it started at the welcome center.

The two lakes would be super nice to kayak on, but not in this cold weather; haha. There we some nice little places to take in the beauty of the Lower Lake on the north side.

In the upper northern corner, the Pioneer Campsite was empty. Visions of summer camp trips as well as Boy Scouts and YMCA Adventure Guides outings ran through my head.

Nice views of the water, too.

Crossing over to the southern side of the lake offered some nice views, too.

I saw at least five deer over the weekend, but was always too slow on the camera. Here are a few tracks as my offering in place of some actual pictures of the deer.

The south side of the lake trail featured a much more classical trail and it hugged the waterline the whole way.

With a runny noise and cold fingers & ears it was time to head back to the trailer and “field trial” the heater; which worked perfectly. Rubi did great and so glad to finally get her to a campsite.
