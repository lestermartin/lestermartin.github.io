Indiana Covered Bridge Loop

2017-07-27 · Lester Martin

I found this Indiana Covered Bridge Loop PDF when planning a work trip to Columbus, IN, and with my love of Covered Bridges I realized immediately it was something I wanted to.  A little more digging surfaced the fact that there are a lot of covered bridges in Indiana.

I broke up visiting all nine of these covered bridges over the three evenings I had available and published the following blog posts of these outings.

  • Columbus to Nashville While Never Leaving Indiana
  • Looooooooooooong Covered Bridges
  • Wrapping Up 10 Covered Bridges in Three Evenings

I was very fortunate to have a cool Destroyer Grey Dodge Challenger to cruise around in on these outings.

I can say that in my week out in the Columbus area, I found Indiana to be much different than I was imagining.  Yes, there's PLENTY of corn fields, but...

  • There are a TON of rivers around here which made me wish I had my kayak with me
  • This place simply isn't only flat farm fields; in fact, much of it reminded me of North Georgia and Tennessee with rolling hills and trees, trees, trees
  • There are MANY areas with ABSOLUTELY NO cell coverage
  • EVERYONE mows their grass religiously – I could not find a single yard, no matter how big, that wasn't as tightly buzzed as a Marine's haircut
  • And...

In #Indiana there's only one brand of motorcycle, @harleydavidson, and only one way to ride it, sans helmet.
