Five Waterfalls Near Lake Rabun in NE Georgia

2019-11-17 · Lester Martin

Waterfalls , WATERFALLS, WATERFALLS; nothing better than a waterfall hike! Today I saw four of them on the Angel Falls Trail and then one more magnificent specimen on the Minnehaha Falls Trail which are on the north and south sides, respectively of Lake Rabun up in northeast Georgia.

Angel Falls Trail

It took a little bit of driving back and forth to start this hike as my guidebook suggested we park at the back of Rayburn Beach Campground #2, but it was closed. Fortunately, we found this trailhead sign just east a bit from there across the street from this MASH-style directional sign.

No complaints as it just make the relatively short hike a bit longer and we got to see EVEN MORE moss (not sure why I love moss so much!).

It took no time at all to find the trailhead we were originally searching for that takes you up Joe Creek.

First Falls

But we did get some great views of first waterfall on Joe Creek, a 20 foot stairstep falls, from the middle of the wooden bridge that crosses the creek at the bottom of it.

On the Trail

Nearby Lake Rayburn Campground was constructed way back when by the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) who also built this trail up to Angel Falls. Early in this hike you find the remnants of an old spring box they constructed to collect their drinking water and to make a cool place to keep their perishable food.

The trail is super wide and well maintained.

Some many things to see along the way – even more moss and other gross growing things!

Panther Falls

We then made it to Panther Falls and despite the low flow of water, it was still quite pretty.

Almost too sunny for a selfie!

Middle Falls

Some steep hiking with some wobbly safety lines then takes you past the middle falls.

While not the most magnificent falls ever, it is still a water and all are worth stopping to soak in the memories and enjoy being out in nature.

Angel Falls

The end of the trail took us to Angel Falls.

The water was coming down softly and the trees have overgrown the rocks. Here is a picture (not mine, unfortunately) of what it looks like at its best.

Minnehaha Falls

Ended the day on a short hike to Minnehaha Falls.

It was the prettiest falls of the day.
