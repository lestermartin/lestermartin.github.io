Interior of Frauenkirche; Munich's Cathedral

2017-04-03 · Lester Martin

While Walking Around Munich the day before I was able to see this huge cathedral in the scenic part of Munich, but after I went in with a coworker (who decided on the fly to attend mass) I stepped back outside (for a good looooooong time ; -).

Patience is when your walking partner through downtown Munich decides it is time to attend mass at the historic & scenic cathedral.

— Earls of Wisdom (@EarlsOfWisdom) April 2, 2017

David Streever rolled into town for Hadoop Summit and despite being exhausted (cuz I'm a heckuva nice guy – and so is he), I took him to Marienplatz for dinner.  Just before we ate we got the chance to check out the interior of the cathedral and take some photos.

It is tall.

It is long.

It is full of beautiful stain glass windows (click on them to see full-sized images).

   

It also has tons of wood carvings and other decorations.

It is a definite stop while you are visiting Munich.
