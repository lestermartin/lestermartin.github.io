Black Rock Mtn SP; Cabin and Ada-Hi Waterfall

2014-11-23 · Lester Martin

We all had such a good time at the 3 T's; Tugaloo, Taccoa & Tallulah, we headed back to our Georgia State Parks for some more family fun.  This time we went to Black Rock Mountain SP where we ended up staying at one of their cabins on this trip.  We arrived late, but were glad to find the cabin in good (and clean) shape.  There was probably just a bit too much wood paneling for everyone's tastes, however.

You really can't see it from the next picture, but the little stream of lights looked like Whoville from where we were at the top of Georgia's highest elevation state park.  Thankfully, nobody felt like the Grinch.

Things looked much better on the porch in the morning.

 

Most of the porch's views were surrounded by trees, but I did find a break in the view when wandering around the cabin.

Gretchen and I let the kids slumber late while we checked out the park.  We noticed our cabin was right in the middle of the Eastern Continental Divide.

I never knew such a thing existed, but according to the wikipedia article at http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Continental_Divide there are multiple "continental divides".  You learn something everyday!  We then headed up to the main overlook area where you can see Clayton, GA, below and Gretchen had to spend one of my hard earned quarters to get a closer look.

 

It really was quite nice.

Zoe even thought it a great place for a selfie (we took this one later in the day as at this time she was still in bed!)

The park offered several other overlooks including this one.

Gretchen and I finally made it to the the attraction I was waiting to see; Ada-Hi Waterfall.  While the trail was very short, the trailhead sign read "MORE DIFFICULT" and it wasn't lying.  It was like a mini Amicalola Falls staircase and steep trail, but we were rewarded with a nice deck at the center of this relatively small waterfall.

Yep, much of it was actually frozen ice!!

A pretty waterfall & a pretty lady!  We even made a selfie!!

 

Again, a very nice simple waterfall and the ice made it a special treat.

I also posted a couple of YouTube videos of it.

 

Gretchen and I then climbed our way back up the short quarter-mile trail.  We took full advantage of the three benches strategically placed after exceptionally tough sections and even took one last break at the base of the final staircase.  We had a fun time together out on the trail, but we will be watching for that "MORE DIFFICULT" badge in the future! 

By this time we were all getting hungry and we had lunch at Mama G's where I highly recommend the lasagna if you ever find yourself up in Clayton.  We burned a little bit of time checking out the old town area before driving back up to the top of the mountain.  That evening we enjoyed roasting hot dogs over the fire and following those up with smores.

All in all, a GREAT start to the Thanksgiving week the kids and Gretchen had off from school.
