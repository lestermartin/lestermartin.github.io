Goodbye Sweet Bella

2014-03-10 · Lester Martin

It seems like so long ago (yet, so recent as all parents know about the time with our children) that Bella entered our lives.

Visions of Bella will always be coupled with images of our kids.

Unfortunately, we had to put Bella down this weekend.  She has been suffering from cancer for several months now and it finally spread into her lymph nodes and she was to the point of having trouble simply eating and drinking.  Bella was a beautiful black lab and she was never mean to anyone.  Now, she surely did scare a few folks with her size and loud barking, but that was just her getting excited in her own way.

She was a great friend to our children and showed them the value of giving unconditional love to a living being.  Fortunately, Bella gave so much love back to them as well.  I'm going to miss them laying with her and cuddling as they all got so much from it.

I am grateful that they decided to not go to the vet with Gretchen and I as it was very tough to go through that process despite the vet approaching it with empathy, kindness and respect.  It would have been too much for them just as it was really too much for Gretchen.

There really is not any more I can say on this terrible event except that Gretchen did a great job on the Smilebox slideshow she created and that she will always be remember in our family as the dog of Zoe and Connor's youth.
