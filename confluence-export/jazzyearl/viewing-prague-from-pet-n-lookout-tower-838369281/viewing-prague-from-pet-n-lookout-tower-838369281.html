Viewing Prague from Petřín Lookout Tower

2018-10-22 · Lester Martin

It has been a year since I was Walking My @$$ Off in Prague, but I’m back again. My same client as last year had me back for another class. Brilliant group of folks out here and extremely pleasant and accommodating. As for the city, I did spend some time checking out many of the same sights as you can’t visit Prague without seeing Prague Castle or the towers at the ends of the Charles Bridge.

This time, I went a bit deeper in the castle and found some “interesting” torture devices.

And, of course, I went down to the Old Town Square for some iconic structures.

The thing that was new for this trip was going up to see the Petřín Tower. The best way to do that is up the funicular railway.

Once at the top, you make it to the Petřín Lookout Tower and the Cathedral of Saint Lawrence.

Very beautiful structures, indeed.

The view was great, too!

And, of course, no better shot for a selfie with the castle in the background!!!
