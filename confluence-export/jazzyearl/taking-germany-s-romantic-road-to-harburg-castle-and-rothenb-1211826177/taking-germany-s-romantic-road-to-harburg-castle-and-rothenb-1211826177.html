Taking Germany’s Romantic Road to Harburg Castle and Rothenburg

2019-04-03 · Lester Martin

No sooner than we returned from the https://martin.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/jazzyearl/blog/2019/04/02/1209761793/Return+to+Heidelberg, we hit the road again. This time, to see a part of the Romantic Road. We did a northern section of this popular road that ends at https://martin.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/jazzyearl/blog/2017/04/07/99817618/Schloss+Neuschwanstein+--+The+Cinderella+Castle. We had two main destinations; Harburg Castle and Rothenburg ob der Tauber. We even had a special surprise outside the walls of Rothenburg!

The entire old town is surrounded by a tall wall which has a great wall walk.

It was on top of the wall that we saw the special treat!! Yes, a COVERED BRIDGE!!

Covered Bridge

What a treat. This is the first of the https://martin.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/jazzyearl/pages/19070989/Covered+Bridges I have ever seen outside the US.

We headed back to Munich at end the third very busy day of our trip, but will never forget this beautiful town.
