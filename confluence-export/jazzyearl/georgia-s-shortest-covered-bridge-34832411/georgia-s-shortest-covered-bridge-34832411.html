Georgia's Shortest Covered Bridge

2014-12-30 · Lester Martin

It has been a while since Gretchen and I went on a Covered Bridges hunt, but we made it out there today.  At 34 feet in length, Lula Covered Bridge is Georgia's shortest covered bridge.  As this article states and our Covered Bridges Google Map of visited covered bridges shows, Lula Covered Bridge is (unfortunately) on private property.  This is what it looks like from the side of the road next to all of the no trespassing signs.
