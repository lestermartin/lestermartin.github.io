For all those who wish to install windows 7 on H500S. It is quite simple. I have figured it out for myself, you would grow old waiting for a response from Lenovo.

On switching on PC press F1 enter Bios. Go to Startup change Boot Priority to Legacy only. Also disable Fast Boot. Save and exit BIOS. On Re-Boot press F12 you will then see your choice of Hard Disk or DVD Rom. Have your Windows 7 Disk in the Drive and select it. Simple and Windows 7 will load and install.

Dont forget to disable fast boot in the BIOS because if you dont your Windows 7 will fail to load basic Drivers like Mouse and Keyboard.  You do not need to update your Bios to perform these operations.

When you have installed Windows 7 leave your BIOS set to Legacy. But you can of course change back to quick boot if you wish.
