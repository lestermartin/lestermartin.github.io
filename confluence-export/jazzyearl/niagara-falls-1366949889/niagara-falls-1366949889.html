Niagara Falls

2019-10-07 · Lester Martin

A work trip to Toronto was the enabler for me to finally see Niagara Falls. It was an overcast day, but I was still able to get some great pictures. First up, Horseshoe Falls which is 187 feet tall and an amazing 2590 feet wide.

IMG_3301.MOVIMG_3275.MOVIMG_3295.MOV

Clearly I was enamored with Horseshoe Falls, but there were great views of the Skylon Tower and the Rainbow Bridge.

Just a bit downstream, the American Falls is no slacker with its 1050 feet width and Bridal Veil Falls (the little sliver on the far right) adds to the beauty.
