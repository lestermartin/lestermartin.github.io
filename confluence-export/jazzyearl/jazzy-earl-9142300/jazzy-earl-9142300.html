Jazzy Earl

2013-05-15 · Lester Martin

Who is this Jazzy Earl?  It is just Lester Martin.  As my pattern, I've got a personal space (you found it) and a professional one; check it out at Lester Martin; Software Development Leader, Architect & Programmer.  

Where did the name Jazzy Earl come from?  A great friend of mine from high school, Daniel Simmons, gave that name to me for my "mountain bike name" as you can see by the stickers on my seat tube of my (now antique) Diamond Back Apex.  I know... there aren't any "mountains" in the DFW area, but "mountain bike" sounds so much cooler than "all terrain bike".  Did I mention that Daniel and I were roommates for a time in college?  He was also gracious enough to be my best man years later at my wedding.  If you don't know him, he's a heckuva nice guy and I hope you get the chance to meet him.

I try to post interesting events in time (at least to me!) to my personal blog as well as maintain a wiki (see nav on left for these links) of things that interest me, so check it out!!
