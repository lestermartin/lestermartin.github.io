Two Waterfalls on the High Shoals Trail

2019-01-06 · Lester Martin

We have had so much rain the last couple of weeks that I was so glad yesterday the sun finally peeked its head out. I quickly checked the weather forecast and realized that the next day would be great as well. What better way to witness the great outdoors after a long series of showers than to see some Waterfalls! I pulled up my trusty Waterfall Hikes of North Georgia book and selected the hike down the High Shoals Trail which has two pretty cool waterfalls; Blue Hole Falls and High Shoals Fall.

The Approach

About 7/10 tenths of a mile in, we found out the first bridge was out. Of course, some budding civil engineer rigged up a temporary solution.

It actually looks like one might be able to make it from that picture above, but I assure you it was much more treacherous than it looks. I assumed our day was done, but then Gretchen started taking her shoes off. I found us a nice wide section to cross where it was not very deep, but it did not take more than a few seconds to realize that this was going to hurt. That water was ice cold! It took a bit of hiking with our dry shoes back on before all the feeling came back into our feet.

Blue Hole Falls

At the one mile point of the hike we made it to observation deck for Blue Hole Falls which is a 30 foot horsetail with a 10 foot tiered fall above it.

Nice spot for a selfie!

Here is a bit of a close up of the upper falls.

Looking downstream you can see the water rushing towards High Shoals Falls.

The Middle

We continued down the trail another 2/10ths of a mile.

High Shoals Falls

We finally reached the observation deck for High Shoals Falls; an incredible 125 foot fan.

It only took a few seconds to realize this was easily one of the top 10 Waterfalls we have ever seen with our own eyes. It is beautiful and was rushing so strong with all the recent rain we have had.

I got the Parrot Bebop drone out for some fun.

I was like a kid in a candy store!! I would get real close and then let off on the controls hoping to hover next to the water, but the wind rushing off the falls shoved my little bird way back to where I was standing. What fun!

I had a little fun with my iPhone’s slow-motion movie capture feature, too!

How about a couple more pictures of this awesome waterfall?!?!

Another great spot for a selfie!

Wrap-Up

We did an about-face and took our time climbing our slowly and steadily back up the trail. At the damaged bridge we opted for a much more narrow strip that was a bit deeper. Our goal was to limit our time in that freezing cold water. Mental note: remember to bring water shoes and a towel for next waterfall hike!

After a total ascent of 950 feet, we were glad to finally get to the car. We spent about 2.5 hours on this outing that only covered 2.8 miles.

We stopped in Helen at Paul’s Margarita Deck for a late, and well deserved, lunch.

Nothing like winding down with 100 of your favorite biker friends and looking down on a section of the S2S - The Chattahoochee River from Source to Sea adventure I’m still chipping away at.
