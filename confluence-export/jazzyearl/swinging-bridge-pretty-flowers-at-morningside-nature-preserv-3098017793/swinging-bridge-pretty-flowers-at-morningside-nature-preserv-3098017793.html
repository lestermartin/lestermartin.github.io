Swinging Bridge & Pretty Flowers at Morningside Nature Preserve

2022-08-07 · Lester Martin

I’m not sure how I haven’t run across Morningside Nature Preserve before, but this little beauty is tucked away ITP (that’s ATL lingo for Inside The Perimeter (Loop 285)).

The vast majority is of the trail is heavily shaded and like many trails in/around Atlanta, it feels like you are far away from everything.

The star attraction of this trail is the swinging bridge and it is solid and well constructed.

But maybe, just maybe, the real-real star was all the pretty things along the way on the trail.

And boy were there a LOT of bees. Here’s a close up of a few of the BIG buzzy beauties.

And, of course, what hike would be complete without me take at least one picture of some shelf fungus!?!?
