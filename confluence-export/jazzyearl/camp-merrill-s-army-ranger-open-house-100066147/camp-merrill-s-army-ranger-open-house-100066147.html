Camp Merrill's Army Ranger Open House

2017-04-22 · Lester Martin

Ever since I posted Army Rangers and Waterfalls I have always wanted to come out to the US Army's Camp Merrill again to see the 5th Ranger Training Battalion's Open House and I finally got to do it this year.  Here's a scan of the brochure of the activities they had lined up for the public.

 

As you can see, there were a number of cool activities, but the highlight was surely the "Rangers in Action" demonstration.  They kicked it off with a parachuting demonstration by SOCOM's Black Daggers team including this precision landing.

My favorite part was definitely the Blackhawk helicopters they used throughout this live demonstration of Ranger might.

I even took a few (very short) videos of them flying around.

   

Can you tell I like helicopters?  It looks like one could get a private license to fly a helicopter for about $10-15K with a couple of schools in the Atlanta area should anyone be looking for an early Christmas present for me.
