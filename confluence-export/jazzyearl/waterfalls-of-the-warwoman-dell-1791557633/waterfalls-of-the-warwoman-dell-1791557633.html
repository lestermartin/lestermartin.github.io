Waterfalls of the Warwoman Dell

2020-05-31 · Lester Martin

Another great day of waterfall hunting. First up was to see Becky Branch Falls and Warwoman Falls.

From the first parking lot at the “Start” identified on the map below, we decided to head across the road towards Becky Branch Falls.

That is when we stumbled on several of these old creepy fish hatcheries.

We found a nice informational sign about William Bartram and imagined being here during the US revolutionary times.

Pretty quickly, we were on the footbridge at the base of the 30 foot tall Becky Branch Falls.

US-GA-Clayton-BeckyBranchFalls.MOV

What a beautiful place for a selfie!

We backtracked and eventually got to the rear parking lot which has a cool old pavilion to gather at.

A quick quarter mile later, you reach the 20 foot Warwoman Falls.

We got back to the car and then as the map above shows, and this blog post details, you can DRIVE a tiny bit and cut a few miles off your hike to make it up to the 40 foot Martins Creek Falls – MY namesake!!

US-GA-Clayton-MartinCreekFalls.MOV

Another good spot for a selfie!!

The map also shows there is a smaller 12 foot Lower Falls on Martins Creek, but the trail was impassable just before it due to a giant tree trunk blocking everything. Not a big deal as we saw some good waterfalls on this trip, but it would have been a big downer to hike those extra miles and not have gotten the chance to see MY Martins Creek Falls above.

Again, a nice day for checking out some waterfalls.
