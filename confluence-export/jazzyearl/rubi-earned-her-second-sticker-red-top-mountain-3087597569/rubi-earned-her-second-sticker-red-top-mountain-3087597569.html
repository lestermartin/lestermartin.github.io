Rubi Earned Her Second Sticker @ Red Top Mountain

2022-05-02 · Lester Martin

Finally got Introducing Rubi - My Coleman Rubicon out for her SECOND trip and started a tradition of adding a sticker from any new campground I take her to. I bought a sticker while on Rubi's 1st Field Trial at Sloppy Floyd's as well and here they are.

They look great on the back of the trailer.

Heck, Rubi herself is looking great; especially out in the forest.

She’s even getting more “comfy” inside as well.

I still have some more little customizations and creature comforts to make to her, but I’m VERY HAPPY with this trailer. This trip I parked Rubi at Campsite #41 at Red Top Mountain State Park.

As you can tell from above, it is a pull-thru spot which I always appreciate. This picture was taken next to the TOTALLY NEW comfort station which was another treat for this glamping trip and shows you how close it is from #41.

You can see the Lake Allatoona just down the hill from the campsite.

On these weekend glamping trips, the Friday night goals are mostly getting everything setup, making dinner and just relaxing. We got setup super quick and decided to walk down the hill to see the sunset along the lake.

Thanks to upgrade #1 (a REAL queen side mattress instead of that super think mattress that came with the camper) I slept very well. It set the mood for all of Saturday which turned out great. Started off heading over to the main visitors center and then hiked the easy Lakeside Trail. If you peek at the trail map you can see there are many more strenuous options, but this trail had some beautiful views.

What was the rest of Saturday like? Well, glamping does involve sitting on your butt and taking in the nature. Oh, and food, too! Made some awesome chicken foil pack dinners. Yes, selfies, too!

And a whole lot of soaking in this beautiful day.

This spot is so good I have campsite #41 (or its sister, #40) reserved four more times this year including right back here in three weeks!
