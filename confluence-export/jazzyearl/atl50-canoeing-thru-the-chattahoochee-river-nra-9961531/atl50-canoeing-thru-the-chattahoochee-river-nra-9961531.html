ATL50 - Canoeing thru the Chattahoochee River NRA

2013-06-13 · Lester Martin

This is one section of the S2S - The Chattahoochee River from Source to Sea epic

The ATL50 was a multi-part canoe journey thru Atlanta in the Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area that took place in 2013. This 50 mile journey is detailed very well in the Park Service's Chattahoochee River map and ATL50; A Retrospective summarizes the trip looking back on it.

The Kayaking & Canoeing Google Map details the completed legs.
