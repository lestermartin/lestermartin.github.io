Anna Ruby Falls

2022-05-15 · Lester Martin

It has been 10 years since I went to see Anna Ruby Falls just outside of Helen, but I snuck back out there in early February when the water was rushing down the rocks. I’ll just leave you with some nice pictures of this merger of two creeks/waterfalls.

The rainbow above was so pretty. The carving (below) of Smokey Bear near the gift shop was “interesting”. They had some super cool postcards for PostCrossers, too.

I surely won’t wait another 10 years to visit this magnificent member of the Waterfalls family!
