Covered Bridges of Indiana County, Pennsylvania

2020-02-11 · Lester Martin

I included an extra day on my first ever trip to Pittsburgh so that I could see The Covered Bridges of Indiana County. That website has all the good details and info on these four bridges, so I’m going to let this post be mostly a picture share of my fun day tooling around in a cool jeep I got as my rental for the week.

I trimmed down my pictures of these four Covered Bridges to just show five pictures of each; two of the outside, some kind of informational sign, one of the inside, and of course, a selfie with(in) each. I hope you enjoy them.
