Waterfalls

2015-04-26 · Lester Martin

I got the bug after moving to Atlanta and now I love a good waterfall hike.  I found a great book, Waterfall Hikes of North Georgia, when I was visiting Georgia's most visited waterfall; Amicalola Falls. This page is devoted to the waterfalls I've been fortunate enough to witness with my own four eyes.  I'm pretty good at new events to create a blog posting for the visits, but I'm also optimistic I will go back in time and replay my prior outings (at least to bring some photos forward).

I’m at the early stages of pulling together this photo album of waterfalls and I’m putting a in front of the waterfall names of those that are in there. It is a bit of a catch up activity right now.

Since I'm living in Georgia I'm listing these waterfalls first, but just below that table will be the other states where I've personally viewed waterfalls.
