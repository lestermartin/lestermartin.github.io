Cabinets & Countertops!

2014-01-25 · Lester Martin

It was another bizzy week for Gretchen and I which didn't allow us a chance to check out the house, but we did take a quick drive over this morning to see if anything had been done since the Drywall is Up posting was published.  We were delighted to see all the downstairs interior doors had been installed and most of the crown molding downstairs was completed.  All of the upstairs doors are in the garage and none of the molding has been tackled up there either.  The fireplace mantle and woodwork has been done, too.

We were thrilled to see the cabinets and countertop installed in the master bathroom.

When then crossed our fingers and went to the kitchen were we were glad to see how nice the cabinets and countertops looked in there (and to check out Gretchen's "farm house" sink).

It is starting to look like an actual house on the inside!  We'll keep y'all posted!!
