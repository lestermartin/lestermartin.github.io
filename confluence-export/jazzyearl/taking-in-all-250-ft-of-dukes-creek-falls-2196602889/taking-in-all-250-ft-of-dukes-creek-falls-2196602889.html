Taking in All 250 ft of Dukes Creek Falls

2020-11-07 · Lester Martin

It was a beautiful fall day on my second visit to see Dukes Creek Falls in the north Georgia mountains. The view from the parking lot at the top of the trail foreshadowed the color waiting on the hike.

The first few hundreds of yards is an ADA accessible boardwalk that ends with an observation deck where you can see the falls down below.

The the gradual decent to the valley floor begins on a generous and well used trail.

And the short hike (and even the non-stop climb on the way out) is well worth it when you get to the bottom to see this magical 150 foot waterfall formed by two creeks coming together.
