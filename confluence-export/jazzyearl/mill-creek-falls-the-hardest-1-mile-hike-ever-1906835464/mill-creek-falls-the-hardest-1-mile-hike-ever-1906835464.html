Mill Creek Falls - The HARDEST 1 Mile Hike; EVER!

2020-07-12 · Lester Martin

When I hear about a one mile hike that will let you see four falls from blog posts like https://www.onlyinyourstate.com/georgia/hickey-gap-short-trail-waterfall-swimming-hole-ga/, I say “let’s roll!” Interestingly enough, it ended up being the hardest one-miler I’ve ever taken. Either the trail was tough or I’m REALLY out of shape. I think it was a bit of both!!!

It started off fine. The small campsite you park at is along Mill Creek.

The trail was well marked and it was easy to get to the first falls which drop 75 feet over a meandering 50 yards.

Continuing on, more cool rocks and musical water.

The trail was still easy-breezy along the creek.

Then, I guess I took a wrong turn or just plan got lost as the trail got much more difficult heading to the main waterfall.

It was getting HOT as the trail ground to a halt, but wait, I can HEAR the waterfall down THERE!

Some backtracking to what looked like where I was supposed to go in the first place popped me out in a big clearing where I realized I was on the top of the 80 foot tall section of the third (and main) falls.

Looking back upstream, you find the 10 foot second falls.

Heading back downstream lets you get pretty close to the edge, but the way it slopes you cannot see the pool at the bottom.

From here, it was easy to see the trail heading downstream, but it clearly was heading down below quickly.

Ahh… there’s the pool below!

About half way down, the trail gets very steep and there are ropes with knots for handholds that help you get down to the pool.

You get a nice view from the sidelines at the half-way point.

Finished the descent and found the fellas I heard from up above taking in the cool water on a hot day.

They were kind enough to let me get a few pictures without them as the stars of my photo shoot; thanks fellas!

You can see half of the swimming pool at the bottom of the falls and you can HEAR the kids who were playing in the other half if you listen to the falls in this video.

US-GA-Crandall-MillCreekFalls.MOV

Just down river another tenth of a mile is the final, and fourth, falls that stand 20 feet tall. By this point I was so hot (yes, I should have jumped in that pool) that it was time to head back on what was somehow a long, hot and hard hike despite the actual short length of it.

One more pic before I grab the ropes and start my journey back up the trail.
