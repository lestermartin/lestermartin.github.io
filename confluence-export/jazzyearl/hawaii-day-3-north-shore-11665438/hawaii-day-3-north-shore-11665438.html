Hawaii - Day 3; North Shore

2013-07-24 · Lester Martin

Today was dedicated to relaxing.  We mostly lounged around Turtle Bay Resort; especially the pool.

There was even a water slide and little waterfall.

We tried some local places for meals; two thumbs up for Ted's.  We also got some more water (and sunburns) at Kuilima Cove.

We finished the day up enjoying Pina Coladas at the pool bar watching the sunset and some fire dancers.

After our whirlwind drive-everywhere Hawaii - Day 2; Honolulu trek, we needed this day.  Mahalo.
